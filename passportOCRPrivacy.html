Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Passport OCR ("Application") for mobile devices, which was created by Utility Apps. The Application specializes in local optical character recognition (OCR) for passports, passport detail parsing, converting scanned images to PDF documents, and offering optional cloud backup integration with Google Drive.

1. Information Collection and Use

Local Data Processing (Core Functionality)

Passport Details, Scans, and PDFs: The Application performs all optical character recognition (OCR), passport detail parsing, and PDF document generation locally on your physical device. Your passport images, extracted details (such as name, passport number, date of birth, expiration date, and nationality), processed documents, and generated PDFs are stored securely within the Application's private sandboxed storage space (using a local Room database).

We do not collect, monitor, store, sell, or transmit your passport content or personal details to external servers owned or controlled by us.

Third-Party Services

The Application utilizes third-party developer services that may collect information used to identify you under their respective terms:

  • Google Play Services: Used to support core Android operational framework features.
  • Google Play Billing: Handles secure payment processing for the Pro Upgrade feature. We do not see or retain your credit card numbers or financial credentials.
  • Google Drive API: If you explicitly enable the cloud synchronization feature, the Application requests authorization to upload your locally generated PDF files and backup data directly into your personal Google Drive account. This communication occurs strictly between your device and Google's official endpoints; no data passes through our infrastructure.

2. Google Drive API Limited Use Disclosure

The Application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Service User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • The Application only uses Google Drive API access to read, write, and manage files created or designated by the Application within your personal Google Drive storage space upon your explicit action.
  • We do not use data retrieved via Google Drive APIs for serving advertisements or for sharing with third parties.

3. Device Permissions Required

To deliver its core features, the Application requests explicit access to the following device hardware and storage modules:

  • Camera System: Required to capture clear photographs of passport documents for immediate local OCR conversion and detail extraction.
  • Storage / Media Access: Required to import existing passport images or PDF files from your device gallery for processing, and to save finalized PDF documents to your local system storage.

4. Data Retention Policy & Deletion

Because your data is managed strictly offline, all application database history, passport records, and documents remain on your physical device.

  • Uninstalling the App: If you uninstall the Application from your smartphone, all locally cached scan history and related operational files will be immediately and permanently deleted by the Android system framework.
  • Google Drive Data: If you enabled Google Drive sync, any documents backed up to your personal Google Drive will remain stored there until you choose to remove them manually.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. Since all operational processing is localized, we possess no technical database access to identify or audit individual user demographic profiles.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any adjustments by posting the updated Privacy Policy document text directly on this hosted URL.
